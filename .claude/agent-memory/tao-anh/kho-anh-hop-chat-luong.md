Tim anh thiet bi cong nghiep (den highbay, den pha, lap dat den chong no) tren Unsplash/Pixabay gan nhu khong co anh hop. Pixabay tra ve phao hoa cho "explosion proof light". Anh kho trong (warehouse, empty) va bon chua (storage tanks) thi co.

**Why:** lan lam bai den-chong-chay-no-250w (2026-11-26) thu 4 tu khoa, chi 1 anh dung duoc (kho trong, D7A6CiIFVk8).

**How to apply:** voi anh thiet bi/lap dat, thu toi da 1-2 tu khoa roi chuyen sang Python (so do, bang so sanh, cac_buoc). Anh khong gian chung (kho, bon chua) thi tim tren Unsplash truoc.
